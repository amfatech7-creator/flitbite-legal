About FlitBite

FlitBite is a native desktop Point-of-Sale and Restaurant ERP application built for restaurants and cafes — menu & billing management, table service, purchases, expenses, sales reporting, and staff accounts, all running locally on the business's own computer.

Google Drive Backup

FlitBite offers an optional feature that lets a business owner connect their own Google account so FlitBite can save encrypted backups of their restaurant's data to their own Google Drive. See our Privacy Policy for full details on what this feature accesses and how your data is handled.
