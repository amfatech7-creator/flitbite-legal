Privacy Policy

FlitBite — Google Drive Backup Feature

Last updated: September 2026

FlitBite is a desktop Point-of-Sale & Restaurant ERP application developed by AmfaTech. This policy explains how the optional Google Drive Backup feature uses your Google account.

What we access

When you connect your Google account, FlitBite requests only the drive.file permission scope. This scope gives FlitBite access only to the files that FlitBite itself creates in your Google Drive. FlitBite cannot see, read, or modify any other file in your Drive — your photos, documents, or anything else you already store there are never touched.

What we do with it

  • FlitBite creates a single encrypted backup file of your restaurant's database (menu, sales, staff, settings) and uploads it to your Google Drive, either when you click "Backup Now" or, if you enable it, once a day automatically.
  • Every backup is encrypted with a password you choose before it ever leaves your computer. AmfaTech never sees this password or the contents of your backup.
  • Old automatic backups are pruned according to the retention setting you choose, to save space in your Drive.

What we don't do

  • We do not share your Google account data, your backup files, or their contents with any third party.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not access any file in your Drive other than the backups FlitBite itself created.

Your control

You can disconnect FlitBite's access to your Google account at any time from within the app (Backup & Restore → Disconnect), or by revoking access directly from your Google Account permissions page. Disconnecting stops all future backups; existing backup files already in your Drive are not deleted and remain fully under your control.
